About Meanwhile

A wider view of history

Meanwhile is an experiment in seeing simultaneity: the technologies, beliefs, movements and ordinary lives unfolding across the world at roughly the same time.

The regions are broad viewing lanes, not claims that cultures inside them were uniform. Dates and locations are often approximate; each item links to a source so you can go further.

Built as a first working prototype. Historical dates are approximate and open to revision as the evidence changes.
